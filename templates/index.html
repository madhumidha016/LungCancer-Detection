Beyond Saliency Maps: Why Counterfactual What-If Matters

Standard Explainable AI (like Grad-CAM) shows where the neural network looked, but cannot prove causality. Our What-If engine performs surgical in-silico interventions to verify decision boundaries.

1. Correlation vs Causality

Heatmaps often highlight confounding features or artifacts. Counterfactual analysis removes or alters the specific suspicious region to prove the network's mathematical dependence on the finding.

2. Transparent Transformations

Users can select between Gaussian blurring, anatomical tissue masking, or opacity attenuation with configurable intensity parameters (Low, Medium, High).

3. True Re-Inference

The modified image is passed through the identical CNN inference pipeline. We compute real probability deltas and test whether the final diagnosis changes from Cancer to Normal.
